Bright

A step for your brighter future. Practice medical emergencies against an AI that asks one question at a time, grades what you say, and keeps going — no fixed script, so each run plays out differently.

Download for Android (opens GitHub releases in a new tab) See how it works

Bright is a practice tool, not medical advice or certified training. AI feedback can be wrong — always follow your official protocols and instructors. In a real emergency, call your local emergency number (119 in Korea).

Features

Built for drilling, not browsing

Every part of Bright is aimed at one thing: answering fast, under pressure, and finding out immediately if you were right.

Scenarios, not scripts

The AI invents each case — cardiac arrest, anaphylaxis, stroke and more — and reveals it one question at a time, in character.

Scored as you go

Every answer gets a 0–10 score and a line of AI feedback on what you missed — specifics, not "good job."

Hands-free voice mode

Speak your answers, hear the case play out. Live captions on screen, no button between turns.

Korean and English

Auto-detects your phone's language on first launch. Switch anytime — the AI answers in whichever you pick.

Your sessions stay on your phone

No account needed and no ads. Your answers go to Groq only to be graded; your history is stored on your device. Details in the Privacy Policy.

Actually monochrome

Black, white, gray — nothing else. Follows your phone's light or dark mode automatically.

How it works

One question, one answer, one score

1

Pick a case

Choose from ten emergencies, shuffle for a random one, or type your own.

2

Answer it

Type or speak your response as the patient's condition unfolds in front of you.

3

Get graded

A score and a specific line of feedback land before the next question does.

4

See it add up

Every session is saved on your phone, with a running average so you can watch yourself improve.

Bring your own free Groq key

Bright runs on Groq's fast inference. Grab a free key from Groq's own console — optionally sign in with Google to save it here so it's easy to find later.
