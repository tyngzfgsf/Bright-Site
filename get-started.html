Delete my data

Permanently deletes your saved key and your sign-in record from Bright. This can't be undone. It doesn't affect the app on your phone or your Groq account.

Your key is stored in a database record tied to your Google account, protected by rules that only let you read or write your own record — not other users, not anonymous requests. You use it by pasting it into Bright's Settings screen on your phone; the site never sends it to Groq or anywhere else. Full details in the Privacy Policy.
